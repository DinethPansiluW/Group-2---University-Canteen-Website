About Taste of Ruhuna

A dedicated portal connecting students and faculty to all Ruhuna campus canteens, providing live menus, status, and information across Wellamadama and Hapugala faculties.

Our Mission

To enhance the university dining experience through transparency, live stock tracking, and accessibility between lectures.

How It Works

Browse canteens, view live stock on every item, compare prices, and order ahead to skip long queues.
